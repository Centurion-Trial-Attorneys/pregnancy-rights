Accessibility statement

Everyone deserves to understand their rights, including people with disabilities. We want this site to work for you.

Our goal

We work to make PregnancyRights.Law usable by as many people as possible, and we aim to follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA. We check color contrast, keyboard access, and screen reader labels when we change the site, and we fix problems when we find them.

What's built in

  • Every page works with a keyboard alone, with a visible focus outline and a "Skip to content" link.
  • Images have text descriptions, and pages use proper headings so screen readers can move through them.
  • Text and background colors meet WCAG AA contrast levels.
  • The site respects your device's "reduce motion" setting.
  • Pages work when you zoom your browser up to 200%.

Accessibility options

Use the round button in the bottom-left corner of any page to:

  • Make text larger
  • Switch to a high-contrast black, white, and yellow display
  • Use a simpler, more readable font
  • Add space between lines and letters
  • Highlight every link
  • Pause animations

Your choices are saved in your browser, so they stay on as you move around the site. These options are extras. They don't replace the tools you already use, like a screen reader or your device's display settings.
