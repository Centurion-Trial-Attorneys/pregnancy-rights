Litigation Counsel

Workers come first

Danielle Rini specializes in claims involving sexual harassment, sexual abuse, and workplace harassment. As a litigator, she aggressively pursues maximum compensation for survivors of workplace abuse, and she has personally recovered millions of dollars for our clients.

She graduated from California State University, Long Beach, with a major in psychology and a minor in criminal justice. Her undergraduate research on the jury rights of people with felony convictions led her to the law. In law school she was a member of the Public Interest Law Foundation, and she graduated cum laude from California Western School of Law.

Outside the office, she enjoys oil painting, Pilates, and time with friends and family.
